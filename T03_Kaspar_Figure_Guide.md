# T0.3 — Suggested figures for Kaspar

**Aim:** Verify that physiology and visual-event timing are placed on a common, hardware-observed HARP clock while explicitly stating what remains inferred.

1. **Clock identity and modality coverage** — `01_cycle_clock_evidence`: classification of per-session DI3 cadence compared with actual SLAP2 `linesPerCycle / lineRateHz`. Report the denominator and reasons for missing comparisons, and separate integer-line from direct DMD-cycle cases.
2. **Representative source-clock versus DI3 detail** — `03_clock_overlay_<session>` plus `04_drift_<session>`: multiscale overlays and accumulation of measured DI3 minus reconstructed *DI3-anchored nominal* SLAP2 pulse times. Use the voltage, glu+Ca²⁺ and early glu examples side by side. Explain prominently that the green pulse origin is imposed, so absolute electrical lag is not identified by the plot.
3. **Acquisition window alignment / clipping** — `06_clipping_<session>` and `06b_processing_clip_<session>`: 1830 vs 1805-type duration comparison; observed HARP epoch windows and actual recorded processing decisions (kept vs discarded samples). Highlight the difference between a display of nested durations and an independently known SLAP2 start in HARP time.
4. **Direct three-clock event evidence** — `09b_three_clock_<session>` and `09c_pd_di3_phase_<session>`: HARP physical analog photodiode waveform, corrected BV predicted transitions, and recorded SLAP2 DI3 pulses. Show a modern example and a historical legacy example; omit when event assets or common raw index origins are missing rather than shifting signals to force visual agreement.
5. **Across-session BV↔HARP accuracy** — `10_population_behavior`: preserved behavior-QC *interval RMSE* (left) is a different quantity from corrected-event-versus-HARP *event residual p95* (middle). The photodiode event residuals quantify fitted event consistency, while legacy periodic cycle ambiguity and potential corrected-event reuse should be discussed; turn on `ANALYZE_ALL_PHOTODIODE` to populate a full distribution, not just the three representatives.
6. **Across-session physical clock stability** — `05_cadence_drift_dataset`, `05b_session_relative_lag_distribution`, and `07_duration_overhang_population`: show drift/jitter prevalence and window-duration differences. These are NOT absolute electrical SLAP2–HARP lag distributions.

**One-frame criterion:** A good BonVision↔HARP fit plus a good source-cycle cadence comparison provides strong timing evidence. The one-frame acceptance claim must also account for source line-to-DI3 epoch/pulse indexing, appropriate display-frame interval, and possible periodic-event aliasing in the legacy alignment. Do not infer false accuracy from the mathematically required pulse coincidence at the anchor.

**Color convention:** SLAP2 nominal source reconstruction (green), HARP DI3 or measured analog photodiode (pink), corrected BonVision predictions (blue). Each exported figure has an adjacent `.txt` describing the precise sources, computations, interpretation and limitations.
