Acquisition-event sampling resolution and DI3 interval jitter

SOURCE: Native index of HARP photodiode.pkl; digital rising edges from HARP_df.csv/DI3

METHOD: Histogram of analog sampling intervals and p95 DI3 inter-rise interval deviation (acquisition-on intervals only).

INTERPRETATION: Characterizes the measurement resolution and regularity of the physical HARP acquisition clock.

LIMITATION: Analog sample interval alone is not PD detection bias; DI3 interval jitter does not prove source-line mapping.
