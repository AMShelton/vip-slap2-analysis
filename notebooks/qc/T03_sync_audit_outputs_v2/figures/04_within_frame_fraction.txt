Proportion of predicted stimulus transitions detected within one frame

SOURCE: Corrected BonVision frame transitions versus HARP analog photodiode edges; estimated or assumed display frame reference

METHOD: Absolute event residuals; each session supplies one fraction; only ≥75% matching-coverage sessions included.

INTERPRETATION: Makes the sub-frame timing consistency claim quantitative without hiding unmatched events.

LIMITATION: Frame values are estimated/assumed, not validated thresholds; fitted-model agreement is not an end-to-end T0.3 pass.
