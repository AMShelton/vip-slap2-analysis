Session-scale stability of stimulus-event timing

SOURCE: Matched corrected BonVision transitions and HARP photodiode edges across each recording

METHOD: Median residual in final versus first quarter of each session; display distribution and dependence on event count.

INTERPRETATION: Quantifies change in behavioral-event timing accuracy through the experiment.

LIMITATION: Does not measure SLAP2 source-sample clock drift; discrete phase ambiguities can still exist.
