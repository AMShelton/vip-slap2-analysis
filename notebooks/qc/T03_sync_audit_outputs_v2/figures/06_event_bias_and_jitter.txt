Event detection precision versus systematic signed bias

SOURCE: BonVision corrected frame timestamps minus HARP photodiode analog rise/edge detections, grouped by method

METHOD: Per session: signed median residual versus p95 absolute residual.

INTERPRETATION: Distinguishes narrow-but-biased timing from both low-bias and low-dispersion alignment.

LIMITATION: The corrected BonVision clock was calibrated using photodiode edges; these are conditional fit residuals.
