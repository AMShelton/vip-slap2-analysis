Example recording: HARP stimulus events in SLAP2 acquisition-cycle time

SOURCE: 863774_2026-08-04_08-12-47: HARP analog photodiode.pkl and HARP_df.csv/DI3, unmodified acquisition indices

METHOD: Subtract nearest DI3 rising-edge time from each HARP photodiode rising-edge time within recorded epochs.

INTERPRETATION: Shows the phase at which each physical display transition occurred relative to the acquisition cycles.

LIMITATION: Nearest-edge phase is not a synchronization error; same-clock index origin and per-DMD line mapping remain to be verified.
