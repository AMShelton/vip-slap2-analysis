SOURCE: Per-session HARP DI3 rising edges and source linesPerCycle/lineRateHz; accepted acquisition epochs.
METHOD: Absolute end-of-epoch DI3 minus nominal source-cycle displacement after first-pulse anchoring; interval departures beyond 0.5×–1.5× nominal.
INTERPRETATION: Characterizes source-cadence plausibility, rate mismatch and unexpected pulse intervals without counting expected recording-window overhang as dropouts.
LIMITATION: End-point displacement is affected by nominal acquisition-rate accuracy and epoch length; not a measured electrical synchronization offset.
