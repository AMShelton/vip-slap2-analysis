SOURCE: HARP photodiode.pkl and HARP_df DI3 rising edges for sessions with available native-clock indices.
METHOD: Median signed event-to-nearest-clock phase and its p95 magnitude per session. Focus-only subset unless full PD scan enabled.
INTERPRETATION: Shows display-to-imaging hardware timing on a common recorded clock, with inter-session variability.
LIMITATION: Phase is not misalignment; absent or nonoverlapping native HARP indices are excluded rather than shifted to match.
