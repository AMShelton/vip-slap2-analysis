HARP photodiode event phase relative to DI3

SOURCE: HARP AnalogInput0 photodiode rise indexes vs HARP DI3 digital rising-edge timestamps from HARP_df.csv (original raw index values)

MEANING: Measures whether display transitions precede or follow nearest SLAP2 acquisition-clock edges within shared HARP timestamps.

LIMITATION: DI3 generally tracks DMD1 cycle edges, not individual DMD1/DMD2 samples. Nearest-pulse phase is bounded/periodic, is not absolute lag, and depends on shared HARP index origin.
